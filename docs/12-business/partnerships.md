# Partnerships

Strategic relationships that de-risk delivery and accelerate the wedges in
[ecosystem-growth](ecosystem-growth.md). A small research team cannot build a civilization-scale
L1 in isolation; partnerships are leverage.

## Partnership categories & rationale

| Category | Who | Why Huxplex needs them | What they get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cryptography / academia** | PQ crypto research groups, universities | vet the suite, build LB-VRF/SSLE/aggregation, cryptanalysis | research access, funding, a live PQ testbed |
| **Security firms** | audit + formal-verification firms | mainnet assurance (mandatory) | engagement revenue, novel PQ/agent-economy targets |
| **PQ standards/infra** | NIST/IETF-adjacent groups, PQ library vendors (`libcrux` etc.) | track standards, multi-vendor crypto agility | adoption signal, feedback |
| **AI / agent platforms** | agent frameworks, AI labs | demand side of the agent economy; Work Visa integration | accountable, settled agent infrastructure |
| **Content provenance** | C2PA / Content Credentials, media orgs | the provenance wedge (human-vs-machine origin) | PQ-durable, decentralized provenance anchoring |
| **Infrastructure** | RPC providers, validators, cloud/bare-metal, HSM vendors | run the network; PQ key custody | validator economics, new market |
| **Other chains** | PQ-curious L1s, identity networks | exportable modules (v4), cross-chain identity | reusable PQ/causal-clock/merit modules |
| **Institutional users** | archives, long-horizon custodians, govs | the quantum-vault wedge; real long-term threat models | quantum-secure settlement/storage |

## Principles for partnerships

1. **Neutrality first.** No partnership grants privileged protocol access, a backdoor, or
   special governance weight. Partners use the same open rules as everyone (Principle #11).
2. **No custodial-bridge partnerships.** Cross-chain via proofs/light-clients only (T19).
3. **Open by default.** Joint work is open-source/open-data where feasible.
4. **Aligned, not extractive.** Avoid deals that trade long-term neutrality for short-term
   capital or hype.
5. **Research-honest.** Don't let a partner's marketing inflate Huxplex's claims (no
   "quantum-proof", no "conscious AI").

## High-leverage early partnerships (sequenced)

- **Phase 0–1**: academic cryptographers (suite review, LB-VRF/aggregation research) + a PQ
  library vendor relationship (multi-vendor agility). These de-risk the defining technology.
- **Phase 2**: security/audit firms + formal-verification group (mainnet gate) + HSM vendor (PQ
  validator key custody).
- **Phase 3**: agent-framework + AI-platform integrations (demand side) + content-provenance
  standards bodies (the provenance wedge).
- **Phase 4**: other chains adopting exportable modules; institutional vault customers.

## What to avoid

- ❌ Exchange/market-maker deals premised on a token launch (premature, off-mission).
- ❌ Exclusive partnerships that compromise credible neutrality.
- ❌ "Logo" partnerships with no real technical substance.
- ❌ Anything requiring a trusted intermediary the protocol can't remove later.

## Measuring partnership value

Real integration / research output / users — *not* announcements. A partnership that ships an
audited LB-VRF or a working provenance integration is worth more than ten MOUs.
